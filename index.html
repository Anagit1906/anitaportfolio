About
A lifetime dedicated to restoring vision

Dr. Anita Arora Khanooja is one of Delhi's most trusted and respected ophthalmologists, currently serving as Senior Eye Surgeon and Medical Director at Delhi Lion's Eye Centre, New Friends Colony, New Delhi. Her career spans institutions across India and the Sultanate of Oman.

Known for her exceptional surgical precision in Phacoemulsification (Phaco) and Small Incision Cataract Surgery (SICS), she has performed over 50,000 surgeries — including some of the most complex cases: hard cataracts, one-eyed patients, congenital and traumatic cataracts, chronic diabetic cases, and posterior polar cataracts.

Beyond the operating theatre, Dr. Khanooja is a deeply humane physician who believes in explaining diagnosis and prognosis to patients with care, clarity, and empathy. Her colleagues and seniors have consistently praised both her surgical precision and her compassionate bedside manner.
